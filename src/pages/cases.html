1. Choose the regimes that apply

    aertun incident regimes eu nis2

    With none set, a new incident starts with no obligations and no clocks.

  2. Declare an incident

    aertun incident declare --title "Token used from an unknown country" --grade sev2 --finding ID

  3. Start the clocks

    A clock starts when somebody decides the organisation is aware, and that decision is recorded with its reason.

    aertun incident decide ID aware --because "..."
aertun incident duties

  4. Discharge or waive each duty

    aertun incident discharge ID "NIS2 early warning" --because "sent to the CSIRT"
aertun incident waive ID "GDPR Article 34" --because "no personal data involved"

  5. Work a playbook

    aertun incident playbooks
aertun incident propose ID account-compromise
aertun incident step ID account-compromise STEP done --because "..."

    The shipped playbooks cover account compromise, phishing, malware on an endpoint, a lost device and exposed data.

  6. Act on another platform, with a second person

    aertun action add okta-suspend-user --org example
aertun incident act ID okta-suspend-user okta:USER --because "..."
aertun incident act-approve ID 1

    The exact request is shown before anybody approves it, and it is sent only when a second person does.

Some reports are timed from an earlier one rather than from a decision — NIS2's final report a month after its notification, DORA's intermediate report 72 hours after the initial notification and its final report a month after that — so their clocks start when the earlier report is recorded as done. Months are counted as EU law counts them: a month from 31 January ends on the last day of February. DORA's initial notification is due four hours after classification as major and no later than 24 hours after awareness. The Cyber Resilience Act's reporting duties (regime cra) apply to manufacturers from 11 September 2026; its final report on an exploited vulnerability runs from aertun incident decide ID mitigated. CIRCIA's 72 hours are shown with a note that reporting is required only once CISA's final rule takes effect.

The deadlines are the published ones, counted from the decision, not legal advice. Notices to the people affected are drafted, planned and sent with aertun notify.
