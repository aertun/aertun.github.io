Connectors

A connector reads one tool through its API, over only the endpoints its manifest declares, with a credential sealed under AeroProve's keyring. With no keyring, no credential can be stored.

aeroprove vault enable
aeroprove connect catalogue
aeroprove connect add okta --org example
aeroprove connect check okta
VAIGIL_CONNECT_SECRET=... aeroprove connect secret okta
aeroprove connect run okta --save

A region is chosen from the connector's own list, never typed as a hostname, so adding a connector cannot point a credential at another machine. The catalogue says which credential each needs and the least it should be allowed to do.

Shipped connectors: Okta, Microsoft Entra ID, GitHub, EVM smart contracts through a Blockscout explorer, KnowBe4, ManageEngine Endpoint Central, ManageEngine MDM Plus and Vanta. aeroprove connect probe shows the shape of a response, never its values.

Collection

aeroprove collect run
aeroprove collect file aws/cloudtrail trail.json
aeroprove collect auto 15m

Logs are mapped to one shape from AWS CloudTrail, Microsoft Entra ID, Microsoft Azure, Google Workspace, Google Cloud, Okta, Duo, CrowdStrike Falcon, Jamf Pro, GitHub, Kubernetes, Cloudflare, Salesforce, Slack, Snowflake, Linux auditd and EVM chains. aeroprove source add adds a mapping of your own, checked against a sample, and aeroprove telemetry check says whether what it produces can be used at all.

Feeds pushed to AeroProve

aeroprove inbound add ssf corp-idp
aeroprove inbound add okta okta-hooks
aeroprove inbound add webhook ci
aeroprove inbound status NAME

A feed's endpoint is /feeds/NAME on the console. Shared Signals (SSF, CAEP, RISC) are verified against the transmitter's keys; webhooks against their secret. What arrives is stored like anything collected, and put to the automations as a signal.
