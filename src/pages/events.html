The event store

aertun spool stats
aertun spool verify
aertun spool retain --apply
aertun spool hold NAME --why "litigation hold"

Retention never deletes what a hold covers, and a hold is lifted by name.

Hunting

aertun telemetry fields events.jsonl
aertun hunt --field raw.user_agent --by actor events.jsonl
aertun triage events.jsonl --rules ./rules

A hunt counts one field's values and shows the rarest, which is what is worth a look. The Hunt screen does the same over the store, and offers what you found as the start of a rule.
