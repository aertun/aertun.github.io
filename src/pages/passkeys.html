Adding a passkey

aeroprove auth enrol sam@example.com --for 24h

It prints a one-time link that adds that person's passkey and does nothing else. Give it to them the way you would give a key; --revoke ends it.

User verification

Every passkey must check it is you, with a PIN, a fingerprint or a face, at registration and at every sign-in (NIST SP 800-63B-4). A security key with no PIN is asked to set one.

Failed sign-ins

Failures lock out the places they come from, not the person: from where you usually sign in, or with your passkey, you still can. After many consecutive failures from anywhere, the method is disabled until the person proves themselves another way or an administrator lets it work again.

aeroprove auth unlock sam@example.com

When every administrator's way in is lost

aeroprove auth recover

Break-glass for the operator of the machine, recorded in the audit log.
