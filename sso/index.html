    Single sign-on

    OpenID Connect and SAML, counted as two factors only when the provider says so.

    In the consoleSingle sign-on

    OpenID Connect#

    vaigil oidc configure
vaigil oidc check
vaigil oidc status

    SAML#

    vaigil saml presets
vaigil saml add
vaigil saml metadata
vaigil saml list

    Two factors#

    A sign-in through the identity provider counts as two factors only when the provider says it used them: for OpenID Connect, the amr and acr claims with a recent auth_time; for SAML, the authentication context. When it does not say, Vaigil asks for the person's passkey as well.
